AEON'S LAW • PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

YOUR PRIVACY.
YOUR RIGHTS.

Your information deserves protection, transparency and respect.

This Privacy Notice explains how personal information is collected, used, stored and shared when you contact Aeon's Law or request housing advocacy support.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable UK data protection law.

01. WHO TO CONTACT

For privacy enquiries, contact:

Aeon's Law
Email: alicia@aeonslaw.org

02. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you contact Aeon's Law or request advocacy support, we may collect:

  • Your full name and contact details, including email address and telephone number.
  • Your housing address and the name of your council, landlord or housing provider.
  • Information about your housing circumstances, complaints, repairs and correspondence.
  • Documents and evidence you choose to provide in connection with your housing case.
  • Your selected advocacy permissions, electronic signature and date of consent.
  • Records of communications and actions undertaken in relation to your enquiry.

Sensitive personal information

Some housing cases may involve information about health, disability, children or other sensitive personal circumstances.

We will only request information that is necessary for the agreed purpose and will apply additional safeguards where required. Please do not send sensitive documents through the initial enquiry form.

03. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Aeon's Law may use your personal information to:

  • Review your request for housing advocacy.
  • Contact you about your enquiry.
  • Assess whether advocacy support can be offered.
  • Communicate with your council, landlord or housing provider where authorised.
  • Help prepare and follow up complaints.
  • Maintain records of agreed advocacy actions.
  • Respond to concerns about our services.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

04. OUR LEGAL BASIS FOR USING INFORMATION

UK data protection law requires an appropriate lawful basis for each purpose for which personal information is used.

Depending on the activity and circumstances, the relevant lawful basis may include taking steps requested before entering into an agreement, legitimate interests, compliance with a legal obligation, or consent where appropriate.

Where special category information, such as health information, is processed, an additional legal condition and appropriate safeguards are required.

Permission for Aeon's Law to communicate with a housing provider is separate from the lawful basis for processing personal data.

Before this service accepts live cases, Aeon's Law will identify and document the specific lawful basis and any additional conditions applying to each processing purpose.

05. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

Aeon's Law will only share personal information where there is an appropriate lawful basis and a legitimate reason connected to the agreed purpose.

Depending on your circumstances and the permissions you have given, this may include:

  • Your local council, landlord or housing provider.
  • Housing complaints teams and relevant ombudsman services.
  • Support organisations or specialist advisers involved in your case, where appropriate.
  • Technology providers used to receive, store and manage enquiries, including Formspree.
  • Public authorities where disclosure is required or otherwise permitted by law.

06. CONFIDENTIALITY

Aeon's Law recognises that housing enquiries may involve private and sensitive personal circumstances.

Information provided for individual advocacy will not be used in public campaigns, media interviews or promotional materials without separate appropriate permission.

Access to case information should be restricted to people who need it for an authorised purpose.

Confidentiality is subject to applicable legal obligations and lawful disclosure requirements.

07. HOW WE STORE YOUR INFORMATION

Aeon's Law uses digital services to receive and manage advocacy enquiries and signed authority forms.

These services may include Formspree, email and other systems used for authorised case management.

Before live casework begins, Aeon's Law will establish appropriate safeguards, including access controls, account security and procedures for handling confidential information.

08. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

Personal information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Different retention periods may apply to initial enquiries, active advocacy cases, signed authorities and closed cases.

Aeon's Law will establish and document these retention periods before accepting live advocacy submissions.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or anonymised, where appropriate.

09. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Some technology providers may process or store personal information outside the United Kingdom.

Aeon's Law will review relevant international transfers and ensure appropriate safeguards are in place before processing live case information.

10. YOUR DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS

Under UK data protection law, you have rights concerning your personal information. Depending on the circumstances, these include:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal information held about you.
  • Correction: Ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of personal information where the law provides this right.
  • Restriction: Ask for processing to be restricted in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Data portability: Request transfer of eligible information where this right applies.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis for processing.

11. HOW TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS

To make a data protection request, please contact:

Aeon's Law
Email: alicia@aeonslaw.org

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

We will respond within the time limits required by applicable data protection law. Normally, this is within one month, subject to permitted extensions.

12. COMPLAINTS ABOUT DATA PROTECTION

If you have concerns about how your personal information is handled, please contact Aeon's Law first so we can consider your concerns.

You also have the right to complain directly to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). You do not have to contact us first.

Visit: www.ico.org.uk
