CONSENT &
AUTHORITY TO ACT.

Your voice. Your decisions. Your authority.

This form allows you to authorise Aeon's Law to provide housing advocacy support and communicate with specified organisations on your behalf.

You remain in control of your case. You may withdraw your authority at any time by contacting alicia@aeonslaw.org.

Aeon's Law provides independent advocacy support. It is not a law firm and does not provide regulated legal services. Signing this form does not guarantee that advocacy support will be accepted.

01. YOUR DETAILS

Please provide your details so we can identify you and confirm your authority.

02. AUTHORITY & PERMISSIONS

I authorise Aeon's Law to act as my housing advocate for the matters I have agreed with them.

Please select the actions you authorise. You may choose more than one.

Important: This authority does not allow Aeon's Law to accept settlements, withdraw complaints, agree to changes to your tenancy or make legal decisions without your separate, explicit approval.

03. DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY

Aeon's Law will use the information you provide to assess and, where agreed, support your housing advocacy case.

This may include your contact details, housing circumstances, correspondence, complaint history and relevant records.

Information will only be shared with organisations where there is an appropriate lawful basis and where necessary for the agreed advocacy work.

Your information will not be used for publicity, media interviews or campaigns without separate permission.

You can withdraw your authority for Aeon's Law to act on your behalf by emailing alicia@aeonslaw.org .

Withdrawing authority will stop future advocacy actions on your behalf, but some records may need to be retained where there is a lawful reason.

Read our Privacy Notice to understand how your information is handled.

04. ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE

By signing below, I confirm that:

  • I am the person named in this form.
  • I understand the permissions I have selected.
  • I authorise Aeon's Law to carry out only those selected activities.
  • I understand that I can withdraw my authority at any time.
  • I understand that I remain responsible for decisions about my housing case.

Your electronic signature records your agreement to the selected permissions. Aeon's Law may contact you to verify your identity and confirm your authority before communicating with third parties.
